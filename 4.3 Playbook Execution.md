## 🎯 Objective

Follow structured incident response playbooks for common attack scenarios.

---

# 📋 Procedure

## 📧 Step 1: Phishing Email Investigation Playbook

> **Purpose:** Investigate suspected phishing emails using a structured response workflow.

### L1 Investigation Workflow

1. Receive the phishing alert.
2. Retrieve the email headers.
3. Analyse:

   * SPF results
   * DKIM results
   * DMARC results

---

### Validate the Sender

Review the following:

* Sender domain age
* MX records
* VirusTotal submission results

---

### Extract Email Indicators

Extract:

* URLs
* Attachments

Submit all extracted artifacts to a malware sandbox for analysis.

### Example Command

```bash
emlAnalyzer -i phishing.eml --header --urls --attachments
```

---

### If Phishing Is Confirmed

Perform the following actions:

* Block the sender domain in the email gateway.
* Remove similar phishing emails from other user mailboxes.
* Notify affected users.
* Reset user credentials if credentials were harvested.

---

## 🛑 Step 2: Ransomware Response Playbook

> **Purpose:** Contain, investigate, and recover from a ransomware incident.

### 🚫 CONTAIN

Immediately isolate the affected host from the network by:

* Disabling the network interface (NIC), or
* Placing the system into Network Access Control (NAC) quarantine.

---

### 🔍 IDENTIFY

Determine the ransomware strain.

Check for available decryptors on:

```text
https://www.nomoreransom.org
```

---

### 💾 PRESERVE

Before performing remediation:

* Capture a memory dump.
* Acquire a forensic disk image.

---

### 🌐 SCOPE

Identify all affected systems by searching the SIEM for matching Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).

---

### 📣 ESCALATE

Notify immediately:

* CISO
* Legal team
* Disaster Recovery (DR) team

---

### 🔄 RECOVER

Restore systems from the most recent known clean backup.

Before restoring:

* Verify backup integrity.

---

## 👤 Step 3: Account Compromise Playbook

> **Purpose:** Respond to a suspected or confirmed account compromise.

### 🔎 DETECT

Investigate indicators such as:

* Impossible travel logins
* Logins originating from Tor or VPN services
* Authentication outside normal business hours

---

### ☎️ VERIFY

Contact the user **by phone**.

> ⚠️ Do **not** use email, as the account may already be compromised.

---

### 🚫 CONTAIN

Disable the affected Active Directory account.

```powershell
Disable-ADAccount -Identity jdoe
```

---

### 🔑 RESET

Perform the following actions:

* Force a password reset.
* Revoke all active sessions and authentication tokens.

---

### 🔍 INVESTIGATE

Review all activity performed during the compromise window, including:

* Email forwarding rules
* OAuth application grants

---

### 🧹 REMEDIATE

Remove any attacker persistence mechanisms, including:

* Malicious inbox rules
* Unauthorized application permissions

---

# ✅ Best Practice Tips

### 📌 Test Playbooks Regularly

Conduct quarterly tabletop exercises to validate and improve playbooks.

---

### 📌 Automate with SOAR

Automate playbook execution using platforms such as:

* Splunk SOAR
* Palo Alto XSOAR

> Automation can reduce **Mean Time to Respond (MTTR)** by **70% or more**.

---

### 📌 Maintain Out-of-Band Communication

Always have an alternative communication channel available, such as:

* Signal
* Telephone

> This is critical if the organization's email environment has been compromised.

---

### 📌 Perform a Post-Incident Review

Conduct a post-incident review within **72 hours** to evaluate:

* What worked well
* What did not work
* Opportunities for improvement

---

### 📌 Version Control Playbooks

Maintain playbooks in a version-controlled repository such as:

* Internal Wiki
* Git Repository

> Playbooks are living documents and should be continuously reviewed and updated.
